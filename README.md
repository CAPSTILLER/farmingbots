# farmingbots - CAPhet AI Bot Arena

Visual spectator arena & simulation engine for 1,000 AI bots in a 10x10x10 nested circular ring hierarchy.

## Features
- **1,000 Active Bots**: 10 Macro Arenas × 10 Sectors × 10 Micro Rings.
- **3 Simulation Modes**:
  - Sideways Cantilever overhang stacking.
  - Twin Stacks Proximity & Touching.
  - 3-Stack Proximity & Touching.
- **Physics Engine**: Center of mass calculations, misbalance jitter scaled by trading volume, and rolling coin returns to center hub.
- **Interactive Spectator**: Clean top-down ortho view, zoom navigation into individual bot rings, and dynamic auto-zoom side view.
- **Color Customization**: Custom coin and deck palette picker.
